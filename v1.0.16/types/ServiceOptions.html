    bot?: boolean

    Enables the chat bot: Team chat messages starting with / (e.g. /weather) are not sent; DashboardEvents."dashboard.BOT_MESSAGE" is emitted instead, and you can answer with Dashboard.replyToBotMessage.

    Once enabled, it stays enabled until the browser tab is closed.
